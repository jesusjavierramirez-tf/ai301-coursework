# Evidence guide: where evidence lives in a plan package

## Diagnosis and grounding

Look at the candidate plan's stated cause and compare it with the behavior and artifacts in the repro-evidence block. Good evidence shows that the proposed cause explains the reproduced behavior rather than merely restating the symptom.

## Scope

Look at the plan's in-scope and out-of-scope statements and the files or areas it proposes to change. Good scope is limited to the reproduced issue and avoids unrelated rewrites.

## Executability

Look at the plan's implementation approach and work sequence, together with the repo-facts block. Good evidence gives a stranger enough information to begin the work without needing unstated setup or decisions.

## Test plan

Look at the plan's test plan and compare it with the repro-evidence block's reproduction steps and artifacts. Good evidence describes an observable test that can distinguish the fixed behavior from the original failure.

## Honesty

Look at the plan's assumptions, unknowns, risks, and deviations. Good evidence distinguishes what the reproduction establishes from what remains uncertain.

## Comms

Look at the plan comment, the issue thread highlights, and the repo-facts block. Good communication follows the repository's contribution conventions and includes any required AI-use disclosure.
